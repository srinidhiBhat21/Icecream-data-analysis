
# 📊 Ice Cream Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

The Ice Cream Sales Analysis dashboard was developed using Microsoft Power BI to evaluate sales performance, profitability, product contribution, and geographical performance.

The dashboard combines multiple datasets and uses DAX measures to provide an interactive view of business performance across different years, months, countries, and cities.

The dashboard focuses on presenting important business metrics in a simple and understandable format.

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard contains four major KPI cards.

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Overall revenue generated |
| Gross Profit | Profit generated from sales |
| Total Cost | Overall product cost |
| Total Units | Total units sold |

Each KPI card also displays:
- Current Year (CY)
- Previous Year (PY)
- Growth Percentage

## 📈 Visualizations Used

### 1. Gross Profit by Category

**Chart:** Doughnut Chart

Compares the gross profit contribution of Special and General product categories.

Purpose:
- Understand category-wise profitability.
- Identify the category contributing the highest gross profit.
- Compare profit distribution.

### 2. Gross Profit by Product Name

**Chart:** Horizontal Bar Chart

Displays the gross profit generated by individual ice cream products.

Purpose:
- Identify top-performing products.
- Compare product profitability.
- Understand which products contribute more to overall gross profit.

### 3. Product-wise Analysis

**Chart:** Decomposition Tree

Explores revenue across different product hierarchy levels:

- Category
- Subcategory
- Product Name

Purpose:
- Drill down into product performance.
- Understand revenue distribution across product segments.
- Explore individual product contributions.

### 4. Gross Profit by Day

**Chart:** Line Chart

Displays daily gross profit trends.

Purpose:
- Understand daily profit fluctuations.
- Identify changes in profitability.
- Compare performance across different days.

### 5. Sales Representative Performance

**Chart:** Table / Matrix

Displays sales representative performance using:

- Sales Rep Name
- Gross Profit
- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Units Sold

Purpose:
- Compare individual sales representatives.
- Evaluate their contribution to business performance.
- Identify high-performing sales representatives.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Filters

The dashboard includes four slicers:

| Slicer | Function |
|---|---|
| Country | Filter sales by country |
| City | Filter sales by city |
| Year | Select individual or multiple years |
| Month Name | Analyse specific months |

These slicers allow users to explore the dashboard dynamically without manually changing the underlying data.

## 🧮 DAX Measures

DAX was used to create calculated measures for business performance analysis.

The main measure groups include:

### Revenue Calculations
- Total Revenue
- Current Year Revenue (CY_Rev)
- Previous Year Revenue (PY_Rev)
- Revenue Growth %

### Profit Calculations
- Gross Profit
- Current Year Profit (CY_Profit)
- Previous Year Profit (PY_Profit)
- Profit Growth %

### Cost Calculations
- Total Cost
- Current Year Cost (CY_Cost)
- Previous Year Cost (PY_Cost)
- Cost Growth %

### Units Calculations
- Total Units
- Current Year Units (CY_Units)
- Previous Year Units (PY_Units)
- Units Growth %

## 📊 Key Dashboard Observations

Based on the overall dashboard view:

- Total Revenue is approximately 6.37M.
- Gross Profit is approximately 4.39M.
- Total Cost is approximately 1.98M.
- Total Units are approximately 205K.
- Special products contribute approximately 64.3% of gross profit.
- General products contribute approximately 35.7%.
- Rocky Road and Salted Caramel are among the top products by gross profit.

These observations provide an overview of product profitability and overall business performance.
