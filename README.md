# 🍦 Ice Cream Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing ice cream sales data using Microsoft Power BI to identify business trends, evaluate product performance, and understand profitability across different geographical locations and time periods.

The dashboard provides insights into revenue, gross profit, total costs, units sold, product categories, sales representatives, and daily profit trends.

The analysis covers sales data from **2014, 2015, and 2016**, bringing together multiple datasets to create an interactive business intelligence dashboard.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profitability.
- Compare current-year and previous-year performance.
- Identify top-performing products based on gross profit.
- Understand the contribution of different product categories.
- Analyse sales performance across countries and cities.
- Evaluate individual sales representative performance.
- Track daily gross profit trends.
- Build an interactive dashboard using Power BI.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard tracks four major business metrics:

- Total Revenue
- Gross Profit
- Total Cost
- Total Units Sold

Additional calculations display current-year values, previous-year values, and growth percentages.

### 2. Year-over-Year Analysis

Compares current-year and previous-year performance across:

- Revenue Growth %
- Profit Growth %
- Cost Growth %
- Units Growth %

### 3. Category-wise Profit Analysis

Analyses the gross profit contribution of two product categories:

- Special
- General

A doughnut chart represents their respective contribution to overall gross profit.

### 4. Product-wise Profit Analysis

Identifies the top-performing ice cream products based on gross profit.

Products analysed include:

- Rocky Road
- Salted Caramel
- Butterscotch
- Almond Joy
- Mango
- Strawberry
- Blueberry
- Tutti Frutti
- Cotton Candy
- Chocolate
- Vanilla

### 5. Product Hierarchy Analysis

Uses a decomposition-style visual to explore revenue across:

- Category
- Subcategory
- Product Name

### 6. Daily Gross Profit Analysis

A line chart helps understand daily gross profit fluctuations and identify variations in profitability over time.

### 7. Sales Representative Performance

A detailed table compares sales representatives based on:

- Gross Profit
- Total Revenue
- Total Cost
- Units Sold

### 8. Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes slicers for:

- Country
- City
- Year
- Month

These filters allow users to explore business performance across different geographical and temporal dimensions.

## 📈 Key Dashboard Insights

In the overall dashboard view:

- Total Revenue: Approximately 6.37M
- Gross Profit: Approximately 4.39M
- Total Cost: Approximately 1.98M
- Total Units Sold: Approximately 205K

Additional observations:

- The Special category contributes a larger share of gross profit than the General category in the overall view.
- Rocky Road and Salted Caramel are among the leading products by gross profit.
- Profitability varies across products, locations, and time periods.
- Sales representative performance can be compared using multiple financial and operational metrics.

*These values represent the dashboard's displayed overall view and may change when slicers are applied.*
